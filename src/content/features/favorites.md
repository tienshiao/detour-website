## What it does

Favorites are icon tiles at the top of the sidebar for the web apps you open all day: mail, chat, calendar, your issue tracker. Each tile keeps its own page open, so clicking one lands you exactly where you left off, and flipping between them is instant.

Favorites belong to a Profile, not a Space. Every Space on that Profile shows the same tiles, and each tile is the same page in all of them: open Slack in Work, switch to Reading, click Slack, and it's the same conversation. A Space on a different Profile, signed in to different accounts, has its own favorites.

Like a pinned tab, a favorite stays on its site. Links to other sites open in a Peek on top of it, and favorites are never archived.

## How to use it

### Add a favorite

1. Drag a tab from the sidebar, or a pinned tab, to the top of the sidebar. If you don't have any favorites yet, a **Drop to add favorite** box appears there while you drag.
2. Drop it. The tab leaves your tab list and becomes a tile, with its page still open.

Favorites are single tabs: folders and splits can't be favorites.

### Open, reset and close

- **Click** a tile to show its page.
- **Double-click** a tile to go back to the address it had when you added it.
- Press <kbd>⌘W</kbd> to close the page. The tile stays, and the next click opens it fresh.

### Rearrange or remove favorites

- Drag tiles left and right to change their order.
- Drag a tile down into your tabs, or above the line into the pinned tabs, to turn it back into a tab there.
- Right-click a tile and choose **Remove from Favorites** to delete it. Its page closes too.

### Shortcuts

| Do this | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Close the favorite's page (the tile stays) | <kbd>⌘W</kbd> |
| Close a Peek | <kbd>⌘W</kbd> or Esc |
