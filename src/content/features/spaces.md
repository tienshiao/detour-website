## What it does

Spaces sort your tabs into separate workspaces: Work, Personal, Reading, a side project. Each Space has its own tabs, pinned tabs and folders, plus an emoji and a color that tints the sidebar, so you can tell at a glance where you are.

A window shows one Space at a time. Switching swaps the whole sidebar, so the tabs you aren't thinking about are out of sight without being closed. When you come back, everything is where you left it.

Each Space uses a **Profile**, which holds its logins and cookies, history and extensions. Put Work and Personal on different Profiles and you can be signed in to two accounts on the same site at once, without logging out or opening another browser. Spaces that don't need separate logins, like Personal and Reading, can share a Profile.

## How to use it

### Create a Space

1. Click **+** at the bottom right of the sidebar. The sidebar turns into the new Space's editor, tinted with its color.
2. Type a name. Detour suggests an emoji to go with it until you pick one yourself.
3. Choose a **Color**, or **Custom Color…** at the end of the grid for any other.
4. Choose an **Icon** from the grid, or click **All Emoji…** (or the emoji tile next to the name) for any emoji.
5. Choose its **Profile**, which holds the Space's logins, history and extensions.
6. Click **Create Space** or press Return. The window switches to the new Space. **Cancel** or Escape leaves everything as it was.

### Switch Spaces

- Swipe left or right with two fingers over the sidebar.
- Click a Space's emoji at the bottom of the sidebar. Hold the pointer over one to see its name.
- Press <kbd>⌥⌘→</kbd> or <kbd>⌥⌘←</kbd> for the next or previous Space, or pick one by name from the **Spaces** menu.

On your first Space, swipe right with two fingers and Archived Tabs slides in from the left: the tabs you closed or that archived themselves, from every Space.

### Move a tab to another Space

Right-click the tab in the sidebar and choose **Move to**, then the Space. The tab goes to the end of that Space's tabs and you stay where you are. If the other Space uses a different Profile, the page reloads with that Profile's logins.

### Edit, reorder or delete a Space

- **Edit:** right-click the Space's emoji at the bottom of the sidebar, or an empty part of the tab list, and choose **Edit Space…**. The same editor opens with the Space's name, color, icon and Profile; click **Done** to keep your changes. Changing the Profile reloads the Space's tabs with the new Profile's logins and history. **Edit › Undo Edit Space** takes it all back.
- **Reorder:** drag Spaces up or down in **Settings › Spaces**, which lists every Space with the Profile it uses.
- **Delete:** right-click the Space's emoji and choose **Delete Space** once its tabs are closed or moved, or select it in **Settings › Spaces** and click **−**, which closes its tabs too. Choose **Edit › Undo Delete Space** in a browser window to bring it back with its tabs. You can't delete your last Space, or one that a Link Route opens links in.

### Shortcuts

| Do this | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Next Space | <kbd>⌥⌘→</kbd> |
| Previous Space | <kbd>⌥⌘←</kbd> |
| New window on this Space | <kbd>⌘N</kbd> |
| Private window (its own throwaway Space) | <kbd>⇧⌘N</kbd> |
